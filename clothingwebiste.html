OUR SARTORIAL PHILOSOPHY

Designed to drift slowly, crafted to outlast generations

AÉRE was conceived as a response to the chaotic acceleration of standard consumerism. We make clothing that embodies breathing room—intentional, spacious patterns and generous premium cuts created from organic, bio-degradable components.

We maintain deep relationships with zero-waste textile micro-mills in Tokyo, Biella, and Lyon, relying on local hands who hold decades of heritage knowledge over mechanized factory lines.
